# Methodology

## Applicability first
Analyze territorial scope, thresholds, roles, geography, data/activity type, and effective/enforcement dates before classifying a requirement as applicable.

## Law vs standard
Use separate labels: LEGAL_REQUIREMENT, CONDITIONAL_REGULATION, CONTRACTUAL_CUSTOMER_REQUIREMENT, VOLUNTARY_STANDARD.

## Source hierarchy
Prefer primary legislation, regulators, government departments, and official standards bodies. Secondary sources may help discovery but should not be the sole authority for an important legal conclusion.

## Freshness
Record source URL, authority, checked date, effective/enforcement date, and standard edition/version.

## Engineering translation
Map every material requirement to product surface, service, storage/data lifecycle, workflow, test, and evidence.

## Uncertainty
Use conditional, unknown, or needs-verification rather than guessing.
